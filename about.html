About Me

My name is Amy Aguirre, and I am currently pursuing my Master of Library and Information Science (MLIS) at San Jose State University. My academic focus centers on understanding how emerging technologies, especially data science, can support research, improve information access, and enhance library services.

Why I Chose Data Science

When I began my MLIS program, I didn’t know much about data science. However, as I encountered the topic across my courses, I became increasingly fascinated by how essential data has become in the information world. Learning data science allows librarians to organize, analyze, and interpret information in meaningful ways that directly support students, researchers, and communities.

My Academic and Research Interests

I am particularly interested in:

  • Data literacy and teaching students how to work with data
  • The ethical use of data within libraries
  • Visualization tools that help communicate complex information
  • Programming languages such as Python and R for analysis
  • Digital scholarship and supporting research using data

What I Hope Visitors Gain from This Guide

I created this guide to help others who may be just beginning their journey into data science, especially fellow MLIS students who may feel intimidated by the technical side of librarianship. My goal is to offer a supportive, approachable resource that explains key concepts, highlights useful tools, and demonstrates how data science can empower library workers in meaningful ways.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope the resources and reflections shared here help you explore the exciting intersection of data and librarianship!
